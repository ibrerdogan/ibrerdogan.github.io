Privacy Policy

Who Let the Gnomes In? · Effective date: November 20, 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Who Let the Gnomes In? (the "Game") collects, why, and what choices you have. The Game is developed by [DEVELOPER NAME] ("we", "us").

Short version: we do not ask for your name, e-mail address, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise location. The Game uses an anonymous device identifier and gameplay statistics to improve the Game, and it shows ads from Google AdMob, which may use your device's advertising identifier only if you allow it.

1. Information the Game collects

WhatWhy
A random, anonymous identifier created by the Game for your device (on iOS it is stored in the device Keychain, so it can stay the same if you reinstall the Game)To count players and sessions and to connect gameplay statistics from the same device. It is not your advertising ID and does not reveal who you are.
Device and app information: platform, device model, operating system version, Game version, language To find bugs and make sure the Game works on your device and in your language.
Gameplay statistics: first install time, last play time, number and length of sessions, day reached, quota and stolen items, rewards, in-game shop purchases made with in-game leaves, daily bonus streak, difficulty settingTo balance the Game's difficulty and economy.

This data is sent to Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore and anonymous Firebase Authentication), operated by Google LLC, and stored on servers in the United States. Like any internet service, Google receives your IP address when the Game connects; we do not store it in our records. Only we can read this data.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game shows banner, interstitial, and optional rewarded ads through Google AdMob. To deliver, measure, and limit ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect device information, IP address, ad interaction data, and — only if you allow tracking — your device's advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS).

  • iOS tracking permission: the Game asks for permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", the advertising identifier is not shared and you will see non-personalized ads. You can change this at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: before any ads load, Google's consent form asks for your choices under the GDPR. You can choose to see only non-personalized ads.
  • Ads are limited to content suitable for a general audience.

More information: How Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google Privacy Policy.

3. Apple Game Center and in-app purchases

If you are signed in to Game Center, your score (days survived) and achievements are sent to Apple and shown under your Game Center profile. The optional "Remove Ads" purchase is processed entirely by Apple; we never see your payment details. These services are governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

4. Data stored only on your device

Your save game, settings, daily bonus progress and purchase status are stored on your device and are not sent to us (except the statistics listed in section 1). Deleting the Game deletes them.

5. What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not collect your name, e-mail, contacts, photos, microphone, camera or precise location.
  • The Game has no chat or user accounts.

6. Data retention

Gameplay statistics are kept for up to 24 months after your last session and then deleted. Google keeps advertising data according to its own policies.

7. Children

The Game is suitable for all ages but is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or US state laws), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or object to the processing of your data. Because the data is anonymous, we may need help to find it: contact us from your device and we will do our best. Our legal bases are your consent (advertising) and our legitimate interest in keeping the Game working and balanced (statistics).

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
